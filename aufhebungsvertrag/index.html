01 — Why it exists

A termination agreement deserves a second look.

Employers often present an Aufhebungsvertrag as a quick, friendly way out. For the employee, a signature can cost more than it seems — and there is rarely much time to think.

Up to 12 weeks without benefits

Signing can trigger a Sperrzeit: up to twelve weeks without unemployment benefit, and a shorter entitlement overall (§ 159 SGB III).

A severance that looks fixed

Employers often present the Abfindung as final. In the individual case it can still be negotiated — together with garden leave, bonus and reference.

Gaps that surface later

Once both sides have signed (§ 623 BGB), the agreement is in force. Typical gaps in the wording often only lead to disputes afterwards.
