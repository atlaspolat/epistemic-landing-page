06 — FAQ

Good questions.

The things law firms ask us most before getting started.

Is client data kept confidential and secure? +

Yes. Conversations are handled with confidentiality in mind, access is restricted, and every interaction is logged for traceability. We configure data handling and retention to match your firm's obligations.

Does Lawsec give legal advice? +

No. Lawsec acts as a secretary — it answers informational and procedural questions, captures intake, and schedules. Anything requiring legal judgment is escalated to a qualified person at your firm, with clear disclaimers to clients.

How long does setup take? +

Most firms are live within a couple of weeks. We configure Lawsec with your firm's information, tone, FAQs, and booking flow, then test thoroughly before going live.

What languages does it support? +

Lawsec can converse in multiple languages and reply in your client's language automatically, so you can serve a broader client base without extra staff.

How is it priced? +

Pricing is based on your firm's volume and channels. Book a demo and we'll put together a clear, transparent plan — no surprises.

What happens with complex matters? +

Lawsec recognizes when something is sensitive or out of scope and hands it to the right person at your firm with a structured summary, so nothing falls through the cracks.
