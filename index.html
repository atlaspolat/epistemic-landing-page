04 — Approach

Careful with the law, careful with people.

We're named Epistemic for a reason: we care about what's actually true. In labour law that means sources, review and restraint.

01

Lawyer-approved

Nothing legal goes live without the lawyer's approval. Every article and ruling is reviewed and published in the lawyer's name.

02

Sources you can check

Every ruling with court, date and case number; every rule with its section. Readers can verify what we write.

03

Information, not advice

The platform informs and connects. Legal advice comes from the lawyer — never from Epistemic and never from the software.

04

Private by default

German servers, first-party analytics, no tracking cookies. People come to us at a hard moment; we treat their data that way.

06 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Email us and we'll get back to you.

Is Epistemic a law firm? +

No. Epistemic LLC builds and operates legal tech. Legal advice on Aufhebungsvertrag-Check is given by an independent, admitted lawyer for labour law — not by Epistemic and not by the software.

What does Aufhebungsvertrag-Check cost employees? +

The first evaluation by phone is free and without obligation. Whether to instruct the lawyer afterwards is up to the employee alone.

Where is the platform available? +

Across Germany, with local pages for individual cities — starting with München.

How do you handle personal data? +

Servers in Germany, first-party analytics and no tracking cookies. The request form asks only for a name and a phone number.

I'm a lawyer for labour law. Can we work together? +

We're open to it. Email contact@epistemic.llc and tell us where you practise.

Do you build legal tech for others? +

Yes. If you work in labour law and need an intake flow, a calculator, local pages or a content review workflow, tell us what you need.
