## Style notes from the Handelsblatt reference

From the Cloudflare/PHP piece:

- **Headline:** `Thema: Nachricht`, about 60 characters. The noun comes first, then the actor and the hard fact.
  Example: *„PHP-Sicherheitslücke: Cloudflare meldet Millionen von Angriffen"*
- **Teaser:** three short sentences:
  1. what happened
  2. why it matters, or a reaction
  3. what the reader learns
- **Lead:** start with the place (e.g. „Erfurt."), then one sentence with the core fact.
- **Body:**
  - paragraphs of 1–4 sentences, no subheadings
  - concrete numbers and named sources
  - jargon explained by example, not by definition
- **Tone:** the narration stays sober. Strong words come only from quotes, never from the writer.
- **Length:** a 3-minute read is about 600–650 words in German.

**Template for each article**

1. Dachzeile: *Arbeitsrecht* / *Steuern* / *Unternehmen*
2. Headline: `Thema: Nachricht` (≤ 70 characters)
3. Teaser: 2–3 sentences
4. Ortsmarke + Kernsatz: who decided what, today's fact
5. Der Fall: the people, the money, the sequence of events (3–4 short paragraphs)
6. Die Entscheidung: what the court held, in plain words, with court, date and Az. once
7. Was das bedeutet: for employees, for HR and for executives (2–3 paragraphs)
8. Schluss: is it final? What's next? What should the reader check in their own contract?

**Avoid:** „Hammer-Urteil", „Achtung!", „Das müssen Sie jetzt wissen", exclamation marks, and invented quotes. Quote only from the court's press release, the published reasons, or a person you actually interviewed.

## The 20

### 1. ArbG Solingen: €415,748 severance cut to zero

**Aufhebungsvertrag: Gericht streicht Abfindung von 415.000 Euro**
*Ein langjähriger Angestellter hatte seinen Aufhebungsvertrag schon unterschrieben. Danach bestellte er über das Einkaufssystem der Firma Bürobedarf und Hardware nach Hause und berief sich auf ein Projekt, das es nicht gab. Das Arbeitsgericht Solingen setzte die Abfindung auf null.*

- **Court:** ArbG Solingen, 15.06.2026, 3 Ca 1629/25. dejure also lists an earlier date in the same case, 14.04.2026; this was probably the hearing where the court took evidence.
- **Facts:**
  - With the company since 2003, about €10,973 gross a month.
  - In a restructuring he signed an Aufhebungsvertrag ending 30.09.2025, with €415,748 gross severance payable in April 2026.
  - **After signing**, and before his last day, he ordered office supplies and hardware to his home through the internal purchasing system. For each order he had to click to confirm a business purpose.
  - When challenged, he said the items were for a project. After hearing witnesses, the court found the project did not exist and that he had tried to disguise private orders as project costs.
  - The sources I could read don't confirm whether he was on garden leave at the time. The value of the goods is not reported.
  - The employer dismissed him without notice on 09.12.2025, after the job had already ended, and refused to pay.
  - He sued for the severance, and the employer counter-claimed.
- **Holding:**
  - The dismissals had no effect, because the Aufhebungsvertrag had already ended the job.
  - But the court adjusted the deal under § 313 BGB (Störung der Geschäftsgrundlage). Honest conduct until the exit date was a basis of the deal.
  - The court called it a "schwerer Verstoß gegen Rücksichtnahmepflichten" and a "gravierender Vertrauensverstoß".
  - The severance fell to €0, which was possible because it had not been paid yet.
  - He also lost a €21,539 bonus: the works agreement lets a bonus lapse after compliance breaches, and the court pointed to intentional conduct (Vorsatz).
  - He kept €9,395 in holiday pay.
  - On the counter-claim, the employer was awarded €834.80 for a business dinner and travel costs that had no business purpose.
  - No criminal charges are reported.
- **Why readers care:** a signed severance is not safe money. The duty of loyalty runs until the last day, including during garden leave.
- **Sources:**
  - [dejure.org/2026,17890](https://dejure.org/2026,17890) (date and Az.; full text not yet online)
  - [juraforum (archived copy)](http://web.archive.org/web/20260728132500/https://www.juraforum.de/news/aufhebungsvertrag-unterschrieben-abfindung-trotzdem-verloren_278520)
  - [jura.cc](https://www.jura.cc/rechtstipps/aufhebungsvertrag-schuetzt-nicht-immer-die-abfindung-pflichtverstoss-kann-zur-anpassung-auf-null-fuehren/)
- **Verification:** secondary. Date and Az. are confirmed on dejure.
  - The detailed facts come from the employee's own lawyer (Dr. Jens Usebach, JURA.CC, article of 04.07.2026), who quotes the court's reasoning. So the written judgment exists, but it has not been published.
  - Checked again on 22.10.2026: dejure says "Volltext liegt noch nicht vor". Open Legal Data has nothing. The NRW court database (NRWE) could not be reached from here.
  - It is unknown whether the case was appealed to LAG Düsseldorf.

### 2. BSG: Sperrzeit after a voluntary exit programme

**Abfindungsprogramme: Wer sich freiwillig meldet, riskiert eine Sperrzeit**
*Eine Bankberaterin nahm 94.500 Euro Abfindung und wechselte in eine Transfergesellschaft. Das Bundessozialgericht sagt: Sie hätte auf die Kündigung warten müssen. Das trifft Freiwilligenprogramme in Banken und Autoindustrie.*

- **Court:** BSG, 07.10.2026, B 11 AL 3/26 R. Lower courts: LSG Schleswig-Holstein L 3 AL 24/23; SG Kiel S 9 AL 10019/21.
- **Facts:**
  - Jobs were being cut in her department at a large bank.
  - She signed up for the voluntary exit list and left on 31.05.2020 under a three-party agreement, with about €94,500 severance.
  - She was in a Transfergesellschaft until 31.01.2021.
  - The Arbeitsagentur imposed a 12-week Sperrzeit from 01.02.2021 and reduced her entitlement by 90 days.
- **Holding:**
  - Her appeal (Revision) was dismissed.
  - When she signed, it was not certain she would be dismissed. Volunteering for a higher payment is not a "wichtiger Grund".
  - Transfer-Kurzarbeitergeld does not change this.
- **Why readers care:** many 2026 restructurings (Autoindustrie, Zulieferer, Banken) rely on exactly this model. Employees who volunteer early, and the HR teams who recruit them, need to know the benefit risk.
- **Sources:**
  - [BSG Terminbericht](https://www.bsg.bund.de/SharedDocs/Verhandlungen/DE/2026/2026_10_07_B_11_AL_03_26_R.html)
  - [evangelisch.de/epd](https://www.evangelisch.de/inhalte/259871/08-10-2026/arbeitslosengeld-sperre-bei-voreiligem-wechsel-transferfirma)
- **Verification:** primary (BSG Terminbericht). The written reasons are not yet published.

### 3. ArbG Iserlohn: severance confiscated, and no job to go back to

**Iserlohner Abfindungsaffäre: Geld weg, Job weg**
*Ein städtischer Angestellter bekam 2019 eine Abfindung von 250.000 Euro. Nach seiner Verurteilung zog der Staat das Geld ein. An seinen alten Arbeitsplatz darf er trotzdem nicht zurück.*

- **Court:** ArbG Iserlohn, 08.07.2026, 1 Ca 1046/25
- **Facts:**
  - Born 1981, severely disabled, pay grade EG 9a, about €4,844 a month.
  - In 2019 he signed an Aufhebungsvertrag with €250,000 gross severance, increased for leaving early.
  - LAG Hamm (6 Sa 903/21) had refused the employer's claim to get the money back.
  - In 2023 the LG Hagen convicted him of aiding the former HR head's breach of trust (Beihilfe zur Untreue) and confiscated €250,000. About €242,000 was taken from his account.
  - In 2025 he rescinded the agreement and sued for his job, back pay and tax damages.
- **Holding:**
  - All claims dismissed.
  - The agreement is not void unless both sides colluded, and he did not know the HR head was breaking the rules.
  - The confiscation is his own risk and gives no right to undo the deal under § 313 BGB.
  - The employer had no duty to warn him about criminal-law risks.
- **Why readers care:** a public-sector "golden handshake" ends with the money gone and no job either. The case is also a warning for anyone approving unusually generous severance.
- **Sources:**
  - [otto-schmidt.de, 21.09.2026](https://www.otto-schmidt.de/news/arbeits-und-sozialrecht/wirksamer-aufhebungsvertrag-trotz-spaterer-einziehung-der-abfindung-2026-09-21.html)
  - [rechtsanwalt-harbou.de](https://www.rechtsanwalt-harbou.de/rechtsnews/wirksamer-aufhebungsvertrag-trotz-spaeterer-einziehung-der-abfindung/)
  - Background: [kliemt.blog on the BGH conviction](https://kliemt.blog/2026/06/30/abfindung-als-straftat-der-bgh-zieht-die-strafrechtliche-reissleine-beim-golden-handshake/)
- **Verification:** secondary (two consistent reports). The 2026 reports do not name the employer; Stadt Iserlohn is clear from the earlier LAG Hamm case. It is unknown whether the case was appealed.

### 4. OLG Celle: the VW–Stadler settlement is void

**Dieselskandal: OLG Celle kippt VW-Vergleich mit Ex-Audi-Chef Stadler**
*4,1 Millionen Euro sollte Rupert Stadler an Volkswagen zahlen, den Großteil aus einer Abfindung. Die gab es aber nur, wenn er nicht verurteilt wird. Das Gericht erklärt die Zustimmung der Hauptversammlung für nichtig.*

- **Court:** OLG Celle, 14.10.2026, 9 U 93/22
- **Facts:**
  - In 2021 the VW AGM approved liability settlements with Stadler (€4.1m) and Winterkorn (€11.2m).
  - About €290m in total was to come back to VW, mostly from D&O insurers.
  - According to dpa, most of Stadler's contribution was to come from a severance payment that applied only if he was not convicted. His fraud conviction has been final since December 2025.
- **Holding:**
  - Shareholders were not given enough information about Stadler's assets to judge the supervisory board's view of what he could pay.
  - The approval is therefore void.
  - Not final: no Revision was allowed, but VW can file a Nichtzulassungsbeschwerde at the BGH. The Winterkorn part has not been decided.
- **Why readers care:** this is the most prominent 2026 case where executive exit money and liability are linked. It is a lesson for every supervisory board drafting an exit or liability deal.
- **Sources:**
  - [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/kanzleien-unternehmen/k/urteil-olg-celle-9-u-93/22-haftungsvergleich-vw-rupert-stadler)
  - [autohaus.de / dpa, 14.10.2026](https://www.autohaus.de/nachrichten/autohersteller/dieselskandal-gericht-kippt-volkswagens-deal-mit-frueherem-audi-chef-3917366)
  - [JUVE](https://www.juve.de/verfahren/olg-celle-kippt-haftungsvergleich-zwischen-vw-und-ex-audi-chef-stadler/)
- **Verification:** secondary. The severance link is reported only by dpa; LTO and JUVE do not mention it. Strictly this is a company-law case, so keep the severance angle as context, not as the holding.

### 5. BAG: senior executives get no Sozialplan money

**Sozialplan: Leitende Angestellte gehen leer aus**
*Ein Zeitungsverlag schließt, der Redaktionsleiter verliert nach 30 Jahren seinen Job. Am 20. Oktober hat das Bundesarbeitsgericht entschieden: Für leitende Angestellte gelten Sozialplan und Nachteilsausgleich nicht.*

- **Court:** BAG, 20.10.2026, 1 AZR 5/26. Lower court: LAG Baden-Württemberg, 22.12.2025, 9 Sa 32/25.
- **Facts (from the LAG stage):**
  - A publisher of a local daily with 28 staff closed in 2024.
  - The claimant had been there since 1994 and head of the local editorial office since 2014, earning €7,857.50 a month.
  - He sought Nachteilsausgleich (§ 113 BetrVG) or, alternatively, Sozialplan severance.
  - The works council had only been told about a €100,000 cap on the Sozialplan.
- **Holding:**
  - The BAG dismissed his appeal (Revision).
  - The LAG had found the employer did not properly inform the works council. It still held that a leitender Angestellter falls outside the BetrVG.
- **Why readers care:** when a site closes, senior managers are often left out of the Sozialplan. Their severance has to be negotiated individually or written into their contract in advance.
- **Sources:**
  - [BAG Sitzungsergebnis](https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/sitzungsergebnis/1-azr-5-26/)
  - [BAG Termin](https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/termin/1-azr-5-26/) (keywords: Nachteilsausgleich, Tendenzbetrieb, leitender Angestellter, Sozialplanabfindung)
- **Verification:** the result and the lower court are primary. The facts come from a law-firm write-up (FGvW) of the LAG decision. **Wait for the BAG reasons or press release before publishing.**

### 6. BAG: agency years don't count for severance

**Werksschließung: Jahre als Leiharbeiterin zählen nicht für die Abfindung**
*Ein Scheinwerferhersteller schloss sein Werk in Thüringen. Eine Mitarbeiterin verlangte knapp 12.000 Euro mehr Abfindung, weil sie vorher schon als Leiharbeiterin dort gearbeitet hatte. Das Bundesarbeitsgericht lehnte ab.*

- **Court:** BAG, 23.07.2026, 6 AZR 34/26 (plus parallel cases 6 AZR 35–42/26 and 46/26)
- **Facts:**
  - The site closed on 31.03.2024.
  - The IG Metall Sozialtarifvertrag paid €10,000 plus 1.5 monthly salaries per started year of service, capped at €220,000.
  - The claimant received €40,096.43 and wanted €11,827.04 more for her 2014–2017 years as an agency worker in the plant's logistics.
- **Holding:**
  - Time with another legal employer (the staffing agency) is not Betriebszugehörigkeit.
  - The separate, lower €5,000 payment for agency workers in the agreement confirms this.
  - All ten appeals failed.
- **Why readers care:** car makers and suppliers took on many agency workers permanently. When plants close, their earlier years count for nothing in the severance formula unless the agreement says so.
- **Source:** [BAG](https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/6-azr-34-26/)
- **Verification:** primary.

### 7. BAG: the standard garden-leave clause is invalid

**Freistellung: Bundesarbeitsgericht kippt Standardklausel im Arbeitsvertrag**
*Fast jeder Arbeitsvertrag erlaubt es, Mitarbeiter nach einer Kündigung nach Hause zu schicken. Diese Pauschalklausel ist unwirksam. Für Trennungsverhandlungen ändert das die Ausgangslage.*

- **Court:** BAG, 25.03.2026, 5 AZR 108/25 (Pressemitteilung 14/26)
- **Facts:**
  - A Gebietsleiter with a company car he could also use privately resigned; his job ended on 30.11.2024.
  - Relying on a clause allowing release after any notice, "gleich von welcher Seite", the employer released him from work and took back the car.
  - He claimed €510 gross a month for August to November 2024.
- **Holding:**
  - A blanket standard (AGB) clause allowing release in every case is invalid under § 307 BGB.
  - Release is still possible in an individual case if the employer has an overriding interest, such as a risk to secrets, competing activity or client poaching.
  - The case was sent back to LAG Niedersachsen.
- **Why readers care:** garden leave becomes something to negotiate, not something to order. Losing the company car can cost the employer money.
- **Sources:**
  - [BAG Pressemitteilung](https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/presse/wirksamkeit-einer-freistellungsklausel-widerruf-der-dienstwagennutzung/)
  - [BAG full text](https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/5-azr-108-25/)
- **Verification:** primary.

### 8. BAG: a removed managing director gets dismissal protection

**Geschäftsführer: Nach der Abberufung gilt der volle Kündigungsschutz**
*Erst abberufen, dann gekündigt: Diese Reihenfolge kann für Unternehmen teuer werden. Das Bundesarbeitsgericht gibt einem früheren GmbH-Geschäftsführer den Schutz eines normalen Arbeitnehmers.*

- **Court:** BAG, 18.06.2026, 2 AZR 89/25
- **Facts:**
  - Hired in April 2021 under an "Anstellungsvertrag" that called itself an "Arbeitsvertrag", and made Geschäftsführer in May 2021.
  - Removed from office on 01.02.2023, then given ordinary notice on 28.06.2023, effective 31.12.2023.
- **Holding:**
  - The exclusion of officers in § 14 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 KSchG no longer applies if the officer role has ended before the notice arrives.
  - The dismissal needed a social justification, there was none, so it was invalid.
- **Why readers care:** for managing directors with an employment contract, the order of removal and notice decides their bargaining power, and so the size of the severance.
- **Source:** [BAG](https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/2-azr-89-25/)
- **Verification:** primary.

### 9. BAG: a settlement form error makes a job permanent

**Vergleich vor Gericht: Ein Formfehler macht den Job unbefristet**
*Ein Kraftwerksbetreiber einigte sich mit einem Techniker auf das Ende seiner Beschäftigung. Doch das Gericht hatte den Vergleich nur festgestellt, nicht selbst vorgeschlagen. Jetzt ist der Mann unbefristet angestellt.*

- **Court:** BAG, 04.03.2026, 7 AZR 297/24
- **Facts:**
  - A skilled worker at a nuclear plant being decommissioned challenged his fixed-term contract.
  - The parties settled: the job would end on 30.06.2023, with paid garden leave until then.
  - The settlement was made under § 278 Abs. 6 ZPO: the parties sent a written proposal and the court merely recorded it.
  - He then challenged this new end date. The employer counter-claimed the garden-leave pay.
- **Holding:**
  - A settlement the court only records is not a "gerichtlicher Vergleich" under § 14 Abs. 1 S. 2 Nr. 8 TzBfG.
  - The fixed term was invalid, the job continues indefinitely, and the counter-claim failed.
- **Why readers care:** written settlements are the standard way to end disputes quickly. If the job ends on a fixed date, the form of the settlement decides whether it is valid.
- **Source:** [BAG](https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/7-azr-297-24/)
- **Verification:** primary.

### 10. BGH: €35,000 severance stays with the debtor

**Privatinsolvenz: Abfindung von 35.000 Euro bleibt beim Schuldner**
*Entscheidend war ein Datum. Die Abfindung wurde vereinbart, kurz nachdem die Abtretung an die Gläubiger ausgelaufen war. Der Bundesgerichtshof stellt klar, wann ein Abfindungsanspruch entsteht.*

- **Court:** BGH, 21.05.2026, IX ZB 45/25
- **Facts:**
  - In a consumer insolvency, his wage assignment to the creditors ran until 11.07.2025.
  - He was dismissed on 26.03.2025, effective 30.06.2025.
  - On 05.08.2025 he settled before the Arbeitsgericht for €35,000 gross.
  - After his debt discharge (Restschuldbefreiung), the trustee wanted the money for the creditors.
- **Holding:**
  - A severance agreed in a court settlement arises only when the settlement is concluded, not on the dismissal or the last working day.
  - It therefore fell outside the assignment period, and the money stays with him.
- **Why readers care:** the timing of a settlement can decide whether creditors get the money or the employee keeps it. That matters to employees, employers and insolvency practitioners.
- **Source:** [BGH full text](https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/IX_ZS/2025/IX_ZB__45-25.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1)
- **Verification:** primary.

### 11. BFH: moving to Malta doesn't save tax on severance

**Abfindung und Wegzug: Der Umzug nach Malta spart keine Steuern**
*Eine Managerin ließ ihre Abfindung ins nächste Jahr verschieben und zog nach Malta. Zum 1. Januar trat ein neues Gesetz in Kraft. Der Bundesfinanzhof sagt: Deutschland darf besteuern.*

- **Court:** BFH, 22.01.2026, VI R 3/24
- **Facts:**
  - A senior employee of an AG agreed on 11.02.2016 to leave on 30.09.2016 against a severance (amount redacted).
  - At her request, payment was moved to January 2017; she moved to Malta in September 2016.
  - § 50d Abs. 12 EStG took effect on 01.01.2017.
- **Holding:**
  - Applying the new rule is permitted "unechte Rückwirkung".
  - She chose the deferral herself and could have protected herself with a tax clause.
  - Germany keeps the right to tax despite the double-taxation treaty with Malta.
- **Why readers care:** for internationally mobile executives, timing the payout and moving abroad is no longer a tax plan. Exit deals should cover changes in tax law.
- **Sources:**
  - [BFH](https://www.bundesfinanzhof.de/en/entscheidungen/entscheidungen-online/decision-detail/STRE202650067/)
  - [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.de/themen/2026/bfh-urteil-besteuerung-von-abfindungen-unter-den-deutschen-doppelbesteuerungsabkommen/)
- **Verification:** primary.

### 12. BFH: no German tax on garden-leave pay for a Swiss resident

**Freistellung über die Grenze: Deutschland darf nicht besteuern**
*Ein Manager lebte in der Schweiz und wurde nach seinem Aufhebungsvertrag freigestellt. Für das Gehalt in dieser Zeit hat Deutschland kein Besteuerungsrecht. Zugleich wird es schwerer, zu viel gezahlte Lohnsteuer zurückzuholen.*

- **Court:** BFH, 09.04.2026, VI R 12/24 (published about July 2026)
- **Facts:**
  - A leitender Angestellter of a German GmbH had lived in Switzerland since 2016.
  - He signed an Aufhebungsvertrag on 17.05.2018, effective 31.12.2018, and was released from work from 18.05.2018 on full pay.
  - He had worked only 47 days in Germany that year, but the employer withheld German wage tax on the whole year.
- **Holding:**
  - Garden-leave pay is not German-source income, because no work was done in Germany. The non-compete during the release does not change this.
  - **Second point:** the BFH changed its case law. Employees no longer have a direct refund claim and must challenge the employer's Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung instead.
- **Why readers care:** this matters for cross-border executives and for payroll departments. The refund route that advisers used for years is gone.
- **Sources:**
  - [BFH](https://www.bundesfinanzhof.de/en/entscheidungen/entscheidungen-online/decision-detail/STRE202610130/)
  - [DIHK](https://www.dihk.de/de/newsroom/bfh-kippt-erstattungsweg-bei-zu-unrecht-einbehaltener-lohnsteuer-184534)
- **Verification:** primary.

### 13. LAG Niedersachsen: severance is due immediately

**Abfindung: Fällig ist sofort, nicht mit der nächsten Gehaltsabrechnung**
*Ein Arbeitgeber wollte eine Abfindung von 38.000 Euro erst mit dem nächsten Lohnlauf überweisen. Das Landesarbeitsgericht Niedersachsen sagt: Das Geld war sofort fällig. Die Kosten der Zwangsvollstreckung trägt der Arbeitgeber.*

- **Court:** LAG Niedersachsen, 07.07.2026, 5 Ta 165/26
- **Facts:**
  - A court settlement on 29.09.2025 gave the employee €38,000 with no payment date; the job had already ended.
  - The employer waited for payroll. Her lawyer threatened enforcement on 16.10.2025, and €423 in costs arose.
- **Holding:**
  - Without an agreed date, the payment is due immediately (§ 271 BGB), allowing at most about two weeks to process it.
  - The employer bears the costs.
- **Why readers care:** it is a small case with a clear lesson. Write the payment date into every settlement.
- **Source:** [otto-schmidt.de, 14.09.2026](https://www.otto-schmidt.de/news/arbeits-und-sozialrecht/wann-wird-die-in-einem-vergleich-vereinbarte-abfindung-fallig-2026-09-14.html)
- **Verification:** secondary.

### 14. LAG Sachsen: a manufactured dismissal costs €30,000

**Konstruierte Kündigung: Gemeinde muss Elektriker 30.000 Euro zahlen**
*Zwei Abmahnungen und eine Kündigung hatte die Gemeinde schon verloren. Dann bestellte sie den Mann mit weniger als einem Tag Vorlauf zur Arbeit und kündigte erneut. Das Gericht löste das Arbeitsverhältnis auf und setzte eine Abfindung fest.*

- **Court:** LAG Sachsen, 24.03.2026, 3 SLa 38/25. Lower court: ArbG Leipzig 7 Ca 1169/24.
- **Facts:**
  - An electrician at a municipal Bauhof since 2002, aged 54.
  - After winning against two warnings and a 2022 dismissal, he was ordered back at less than 24 hours' notice and dismissed again on 22.03.2024.
- **Holding:**
  - The court saw an obvious attempt to manufacture a dismissal reason.
  - On the employee's request (Auflösungsantrag, § 9 KSchG) it dissolved the employment against €30,000 gross.
  - That is about half the statutory cap of €57,347.70. The amount was reduced because he found an equivalent job immediately.
- **Why readers care:** it shows how courts set severance when the employer behaves badly. Manufacturing a reason does not save money.
- **Source:** [iww.de (judgment text)](https://www.iww.de/quellenmaterial/id/255077)
- **Verification:** primary (reproduced judgment).

### 15. ArbG Heilbronn: a non-compete in the exit deal blocks a move to a supplier

**Aufhebungsvertrag: Wettbewerbsverbot stoppt Wechsel zum Lieferanten**
*Ein Einkaufsmanager unterschrieb seinen Aufhebungsvertrag samt Wettbewerbsverbot und hatte den neuen Vertrag beim Lieferanten schon in der Tasche. Das Arbeitsgericht Heilbronn ließ ihn nicht per Eilverfahren wechseln.*

- **Court:** ArbG Heilbronn, 12.02.2026, 8 Ga 1/26
- **Facts:**
  - A Senior Purchasing Manager (€130,200 a year) at an international retail group.
  - He signed an Aufhebungsvertrag on 20.08.2025, effective 28.02.2026, which re-confirmed a 12-month post-contractual non-compete.
  - He had already signed with a supplier he had negotiated with, starting 01.03.2026, and asked for an injunction allowing the move.
- **Holding:**
  - The application was dismissed for lack of urgency.
  - It counted against him that he had not raised his plans when signing.
- **Why readers care:** the non-compete clause is often treated as boilerplate in exit negotiations. This shows what it can cost.
- **Source:** [openlegaldata (full text)](https://de.openlegaldata.io/api/cases/418203/)
- **Verification:** primary.

### 16. LAG Hamm and BAG: reference clauses in settlements

**Arbeitszeugnis im Vergleich: Wer zusagt, muss liefern**
*Im Vergleich hatte der Arbeitgeber ein gutes Zeugnis versprochen. Später verwies er auf eine „katastrophale Leistung" und einen verschlafenen Workshop. Das kostet 1.000 Euro Zwangsgeld, doch das Bundesarbeitsgericht setzt Grenzen.*

- **Courts:**
  - LAG Hamm, 17.09.2026, 9 Ta 209/26
  - BAG, 07.05.2026, 8 AZB 25/25
- **Facts:**
  - LAG Hamm: a settlement promised an ERP consultant (born 1960) a reference graded "stets zur vollen Zufriedenheit" and a right to request changes. The employer refused, citing poor performance and him oversleeping in a workshop after a heavy evening.
  - BAG: a hospital company's managing director had a right in his settlement to submit a draft reference.
- **Holding:**
  - LAG Hamm upheld the €1,000 Zwangsgeld; the clause can be enforced.
  - BAG: draft-right clauses can be enforced, but if the employer plausibly argues the draft is untrue, there is no Zwangsgeld and the dispute needs new proceedings.
- **Why readers care:** the reference clause is part of almost every exit deal. Together the two rulings show what it is worth.
- **Sources:**
  - [LAG Hamm (openlegaldata)](https://de.openlegaldata.io/api/cases/521987/)
  - [BAG](https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/8-azb-25-25/)
- **Verification:** primary for both. Read both texts side by side, because they seem to pull in different directions.

### 17. BAG: Sozialplan severance unpaid after 13 years

**Insolvenz: Seit 13 Jahren wartet ein Arbeitnehmer auf seine Abfindung**
*2013 stand ihm laut Sozialplan eine Abfindung von 5.380 Euro zu. Gezahlt wurde bis heute nicht. Das Bundesarbeitsgericht erklärt, warum der Anspruch weiter besteht und warum er trotzdem warten muss.*

- **Court:** BAG, 02.06.2026, 1 AZR 166/25 (plus 167/25 and 168/25). Related case pending: 6 AZR 154–157/25, hearing 19.11.2026.
- **Facts:**
  - Insolvency began in February 2013, and the estate was declared insufficient (Masseunzulänglichkeit).
  - A Sozialplan of 28.02.2013 for 25 employees promised him €5,380.50, payable only once the insufficiency was clearly removed.
  - In January 2014 the administrator told the court it had ended, but did not tell the employees.
- **Holding:**
  - § 123 InsO only blocks payment; it does not reduce the claim.
  - The claim falls due at the earliest after the final hearing (Schlusstermin).
  - Whether it is due, forfeited or time-barred goes back to LAG Hamm.
- **Why readers care:** in an insolvency, a Sozialplan figure on paper is not money in the account.
- **Source:** [BAG](https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/1-azr-166-25/)
- **Verification:** primary.

### 18. FG Münster: the Fünftelregelung misunderstanding

**Fünftelregelung: Ein verbreiteter Irrtum führt zur Steuernachzahlung**
*Ein Arbeitnehmer glaubte, seine Abfindung werde über fünf Jahre verteilt besteuert. Der Finanzbeamte glaubte das auch. Das Finanzgericht Münster erlaubte die spätere Korrektur, und der Mann muss nachzahlen.*

- **Court:** FG Münster, 13.02.2026, 4 K 64/23 E. Revision pending at the BFH: IX R 3/26.
- **Facts:**
  - The severance was paid in January 2019.
  - Taxpayer and tax officer both assumed the Fünftelregelung spreads the payment over five years, so only one fifth was taxed in 2019.
  - The error was found while processing 2020.
- **Holding:** the 2019 assessment could be corrected to tax the full sum at the reduced § 34 EStG rate (§§ 175b, 174 Abs. 3 AO).
- **Why readers care:** since 2025, employers no longer apply the Fünftelregelung through payroll; it is applied only in the tax return. Mistakes like this one now surface later and cost more.
- **Sources:**
  - [NWB](https://datenbank.nwb.de/Dokument/1091950/)
  - [Haufe, pending BFH cases May 2026](https://www.haufe.de/steuern/rechtsprechung/bfh-neue-anhaengige-verfahren-im-mai-2026_166_687584.html)
- **Verification:** secondary. The NRWE full text could not be reached.

### 19. LAG Köln: a typo in an exit contract binds the carmaker *(flag: decided 19.11.2025, published January 2026)*

**Aufhebungsvertrag: Ein „Fehlerteufel" kostet einen Autobauer viel Geld**
*4.118 Euro im Monat bis zur Rente standen im Vertrag. Gemeint waren angeblich nur 981 Euro. Das Landesarbeitsgericht Köln sagt: Es gilt, was unterschrieben ist.*

- **Court:** LAG Köln, 19.11.2025, 4 SLa 276/25
- **Facts:**
  - A carmaker cutting jobs offered a bridging model (Überbrückungsmodell) until retirement in 2031.
  - The signed contract promised €4,118.42 gross a month in every phase.
  - HR later emailed that a "Fehlerteufel" had crept in: phase 2 (10/2024–11/2025) should be €981.02. It cut the payments.
- **Holding:**
  - The clear wording wins.
  - The challenge (Anfechtung) was not made without delay, and a pay term cannot be challenged on its own.
  - By our own calculation, not stated in the sources, the employer owes about €44,000 gross more to this one employee.
- **Why readers care:** in mass-redundancy programmes, one error in a template is copied into hundreds of contracts.
- **Sources:**
  - [Haufe](https://www.haufe.de/personal/arbeitsrecht/fehler-im-aufhebungsvertrag_76_671088.html)
  - [otto-schmidt.de](https://www.otto-schmidt.de/news/arbeits-und-sozialrecht/auslegung-eines-aufhebungsvertrags-falsa-demonstratio-2026-01-09.html)
- **Verification:** secondary (three consistent reports). The company is not named.

### 20. BAG, hearing 17 November: a Sozialplan after a late works council election *(pending)*

**Bankschließung: Kommt der Sozialplan auch nach der späten Betriebsratswahl?**
*Erst kündigte die Bank die Schließung an, dann wählten die Beschäftigten einen Betriebsrat. Am 17. November entscheidet das Bundesarbeitsgericht, ob sie noch einen Sozialplan erzwingen können.*

- **Court:** BAG, 1 ABR 4/26, hearing 17.11.2026 (moved from 20.10.2026). Lower court: LAG Düsseldorf 3 TaBV 39/25.
- **Facts:**
  - The German branch of an international bank in Düsseldorf was to close.
  - Staff were told on 19.10.2023. In early November the bank announced severance payments and loyalty bonuses.
  - A works council was elected on 01.12.2023, and the bank refused a Sozialplan. The branch closed on 30.06.2025.
  - Both lower courts said a works council elected after the closure decision cannot force one.
- **Why readers care:** foreign banks and companies close German units regularly. The ruling decides whether staff can still win Sozialplan money by organising late.
- **Source:** [BAG preview](https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/termin/1-abr-4-26/)
- **Verification:** primary (preview). **Publish as a preview before 17.11., or as news after the ruling.**
